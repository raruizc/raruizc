# Hi, I'm Ricardo 👋

**Statistician and plant breeding data scientist.** I turn field-trial and genomic data into better selection decisions using mixed models, Bayesian statistics, information theory and machine learning.

- 🎓 PhD candidate in **Statistics and Agricultural Experimentation**, ESALQ/USP (Brazil)
- 🌾 Agricultural Engineer · MSc in **Genetics and Plant Breeding** (UFLA)
- 🏆 1st place (best ESALQ project) in the **GDM Universities Challenge** with [MIBLUP](https://github.com/raruizc/MIBLUP)
- 📍 Bogotá, Colombia · Piracicaba, Brazil · 🗣️ Spanish · Portuguese · English

## 🔬 What I work on

| Area | Methods |
|---|---|
| **Quantitative genetics** | Genomic selection (GBLUP), G×E, multi-environment trials, factor-analytic models, stability (GGE, MTSI) |
| **Statistical modeling** | Linear mixed models (ASReml, lme4), Bayesian hierarchical models (Stan), spatial field-trial analysis |
| **Information theory** | Mutual information and Shannon entropy for marker selection and stability |
| **Time series & spatial** | ARIMA/SARIMA, Prophet, geostatistics/kriging, NDVI from remote sensing |
| **Machine learning** | CNNs for plant disease detection, Random Forest, clustering, PCA, Factor Analysis, GLM |

## ⭐ Featured projects

- [**MIBLUP**](https://github.com/raruizc/MIBLUP): award-winning, mutual-information-weighted genomic selection
- [**nordic-barley-fa-mi**](https://github.com/raruizc/nordic-barley-fa-mi): factor-analytic models + mutual information in Nordic barley multi-environment trials
- [**Wheat Bayesian + Information Theory**](https://github.com/raruizc/LCF5900_Wheat-Bayesian-Infotheory_Breeding): Bayesian G×E and multi-trait selection
- [**Tomato Disease Identification**](https://github.com/raruizc/DiseaseIdentification): CNN classifier, 10 classes, ~89% validation accuracy
- [**Tahiti Lemon Price Analysis**](https://github.com/raruizc/TahitiLemonPriceAnalysis): ARIMA, SARIMA analysis in volatility serialtime prices
- [**Climate Time Series Piracicaba**](https://github.com/raruizc/ClimateTimeSeriesPiracicaba): ENSO effects on extreme weather, 1950–2026
